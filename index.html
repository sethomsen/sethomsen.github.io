About Me

I am a Senior Cryptographic Engineer at Partisia, where I work on applied cryptography — primarily multi-party computation, zero-knowledge proofs, and blockchain protocols. More recently I have become increasingly interested in digital identity: how people can prove things about themselves, share credentials, and interact with institutions without giving up more information than strictly necessary. This has drawn me into self-sovereign identity systems and the technical standards being developed for the EU Digital Identity Wallet.

Before joining Partisia, I worked at the Alexandra Institute, where I was involved in designing privacy-enhancing systems and carrying out security analyses.

I completed my PhD in computer science at Aarhus University, supervised by Jesper Buus Nielsen, where I also did my undergraduate studies. My research focused on the security of peer-to-peer networks and consensus protocols in the blockchain setting.
